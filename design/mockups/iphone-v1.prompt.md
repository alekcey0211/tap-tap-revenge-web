# iPhone UI concept v1

Mode: built-in image generation. Use case: ui-mockup.
One presentation board: track selection, gameplay and results.
Status: design proposal; no gameplay implementation or approval implied.

## Prompt

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished high-resolution UI design presentation board containing THREE complete, flat, front-facing iPhone app screens side by side, equal size, aligned at top and bottom, showing a coherent redesign of a three-lane rhythm game. Landscape board roughly 3:2. Each individual screen has a tall iPhone 390x844 aspect ratio, subtly rounded screen corners, no 3D phone hardware, no hands, no perspective. Neutral very dark charcoal board, generous clean gaps, small external captions above each screen: "01 / ВЫБОР ТРЕКА", "02 / ИГРА", "03 / РЕЗУЛЬТАТ". Under the board a tiny caption: "КОНЦЕПТ / iPHONE · ДАННЫЕ ДЛЯ ПРИМЕРА". Screens must be large and readable, occupying most of the canvas.

Shared art direction: Album Art meets Neon Stage. Sophisticated energetic music game, original visual identity, not a generic dashboard or sci-fi control panel. Near-black warm graphite #101113, ivory typography #F3F2EE, vibrant acid-lime #C9FF56 primary action color. Three permanent gameplay lane colors: coral-red, lime-green, electric cyan. Localized controlled glow only on active notes and hit rings, not on all text. Strong typography hierarchy, clean contemporary sans-serif, tasteful negative space, restrained thin dividers, generous touch targets, unified 20px horizontal screen padding. NO chrome textures, skeuomorphic metal, rainbow gradients, purple glassmorphism, giant decorative logos or invented app name. Dark minimal iOS status bars at 9:41 and safe bottom home indicators; keep tappable controls above safe area. Russian UI text must be clean and correctly spelled. Song and artist names remain English. Do not include music generation, AI badges, login, purchases, achievements, Pulse mechanic or nonexistent social features.

SCREEN 1, TRACK SELECTION:
Top app header "Музыка" large bold left, one simple settings gear icon right.
Small muted context "3 трека · 12 карт".
Featured selected song: a crisp large square ORIGINAL abstract lime-and-black album illustration (angular sound-wave ribbons / poster-like club artwork, not an imitation of an existing commercial cover). Below it text "House Music" prominent, "Benny Benassi" smaller, compact "2:09 · Dance".
Below selected hero, a clean compact two-row track switcher with small abstract thumbnail images: "Waiting for the End" / "Linkin Park", then "Sing" / "My Chemical Romance". These rows must not compete with the featured song. Visual indication that House Music is selected is on the featured card.
Section caption "СЛОЖНОСТЬ". Four segmented options: "Легко", "Средне", "Сложно", "Экстрим"; "Средне" highlighted with lime. Small record line "Рекорд 271 800".
Large solid lime full-width bottom button with dark text and small play icon: "Играть".
Everything fits naturally on one screen without tiny text; no extra navbar or filler panels.

SCREEN 2, GAMEPLAY:
Same graphite app. Compact top header: small matching lime abstract square cover, "House Music", small "Benny Benassi", accessible pause icon right.
Below header a clear HUD: large white score "78 240" left, small "КОМБО" and "128" right. Thin song progress line and small "0:42 / 2:09". No giant combo text in the middle of the playfield.
Most screen area occupied by exactly THREE clean perspective rhythm lanes, red left, lime middle, cyan right. Lanes converge gently toward the upper central horizon but NEVER cross or change ordering. Very subtle atmospheric depth, spacious nearly black background. Bright clearly separated circular or rounded-diamond note heads and one elegant cyan hold ribbon. Notes MUST sit precisely on the three rails. Several well-spaced falling notes, not a wall of clutter.
At lower screen, three generous luminous circular hit targets corresponding to lanes, aligned horizontally, safely above home indicator. One compact "PERFECT" feedback located just above the hit line, not overlapping an approaching note. Tiny multiplier "×4" unobtrusive at edge of field.
Below or alongside the field, one compact thumb-accessible "REVENGE ×8" button with a small partially filled energy meter. Keep room for it, no overlap with hit targets or home bar. No D/F/J keyboard labels on the mobile layout. No navigation bar during play.

SCREEN 3, RESULTS:
Compact top back chevron with "Результат". Medium square cover using EXACT SAME abstract lime artwork as screen 1. "House Music", "Benny Benassi · Средне".
Small lime label "НОВЫЙ РЕКОРД"; dominant beautifully spaced score "284 600"; small secondary "+12 800 к рекорду".
Elegant restrained "FULL COMBO" designation, not a new medal system.
Two clean metric columns: "97,9%" with "Точность", "408" with "Макс. комбо". A quiet third line "0 промахов". No fake accuracy timeline.
A few extremely restrained lime celebratory particles only in the upper background; keep typography crisp.
At bottom large solid lime button "Ещё раз", beneath it an understated secondary button "Выбрать трек". Comfortable safe bottom spacing.

This is a high-fidelity design proposal, not a screenshot of a shipped app. Make all three screens harmonious, plausible to implement in HTML/CSS/Canvas, visually fresh, readable on an actual iPhone, and complete. Prioritize finished UI design craft over visual noise.
```
